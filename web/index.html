Join a game

Rooms are private to whoever has the link. Unused links expire after 15 minutes, and a room closes once the match starts. The first room after a quiet spell can take a few seconds while the server wakes up.

Against the AI: press Play, choose Skirmish from the game menu, then pick your map, opponents and starting credits.

Against a friend: use Play with a friend above. Both of you need this page open in a desktop browser.

Leaving a match: to play again after exiting, refresh the page.

Is this official?

No. It's an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts or the Vanilla Conquer team.

Do I need to own the game?

No. The page loads the freeware game data for you. If you prefer, you can use your own files instead, and they never leave your computer.

Does it save my games?

Not yet. Saved games are on the list for a future version.

What about the campaigns?

Planned for a later version. This release covers skirmish against the AI and private multiplayer rooms.

Classic RTS runs the 1996 game's own code in your browser. It's built on Vanilla Conquer, an open-source update of the engine Electronic Arts released under the GPL, and compiled to WebAssembly. There's no emulator involved.
